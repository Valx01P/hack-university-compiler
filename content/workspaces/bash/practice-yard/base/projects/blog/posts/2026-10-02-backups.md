# Backups we have actually restored

A backup you have never restored is a hope. Once a month we pick a site,
restore last night's backup into a fresh folder, and compare the two.

- `tar` makes the archive.
- `sha256sum` proves it did not change on the way.
- `diff -r` proves the restore matches the original.

Twice this year the drill found a broken backup before a customer did.
