# About Trailhead

Founded in 2019 by two trail runners who were tired of gear that gave out before they did, Trailhead started as a weekend stall at a Boulder farmers market.

Today we stock a short list of packs, tents, layers, and camp kitchens that our team has carried on real trips. If something does not earn its place in our own packs, it does not go on the shelf.

We ship from Denver and Portland, and unused gear can come back within 30 days.
